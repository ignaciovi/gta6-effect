The GTA VI Effect

How does the world change when the most anticipated game ever drops?
I've seen many posts online about people taking days off, joking about disappearing from the world. How much of that will be true? I wanted to find out, so I built this website to track this with real data.

Launch countdown --
Range

Productivity

I expect less work to get done: fewer GitHub pull requests and lower US internet traffic. I don't think searches for calling in sick will change at launch, as people will have searched for that before the release. I do expect more searches for food, as people order in instead of cooking.

Attention share

I expect GTA VI to take over the internet on launch day: a big share of the top Twitch streams, trending searches and popular YouTube videos will be about it. Steam and GTA V player numbers should dip as players move to their PS5 or Xbox.

Money

I expect Take-Two’s share price to move on launch and early sales news, while Sony and Microsoft barely react, since GTA VI is a small part of their business.

Baselines = the average at the same hour on pre-launch days of the same type (weekday/weekend, US Eastern time). Search and app-rank data are proxies, not measurements.
